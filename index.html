The Journey

Stop by Stop

Four stops. Each one completely different. Here's how it went.

Big Sur

COAST

Highway 1 north from the bottom. Sand Dollar Beach, McWay Falls, Bixby Bridge at golden hour, Point Lobos. Ended the day at Garrapata State Beach — a wedding photoshoot happening in the field, a dog climbing rocks, and a sunset that made everyone put their phones away.

Highway 1 Sand Dollar Beach Garrapata Sunset

Carmel → Yosemite

SIERRA

Early Carmel stop for coffee (not worth it). Casa De Fruta on the way in — fresh fruit, dried goods, a carousel, and a train ride. Into Yosemite for Tunnel View, Bridalveil Fall, El Capitan Meadow, and a drive along the Merced River. The sunset drive out through Tunnel View was the moment.

Casa De Fruta Tunnel View El Capitan

Mist Trail

SIERRA

Mist Trail up to Vernal Falls. Made it to the top — then found out the loop was closed. Backtracked the same steep, wet path back down. Calzone Pockets for dinner. Absolutely earned it.

Mist Trail Vernal Falls Loop Closed ↩

St Helena, Napa Valley

WINE COUNTRY

Wine Passport in hand. Four vineyards, one afternoon. The kind of day that was never in the original plan and ended up being everyone's favourite.

Wine Passport 4 Vineyards $95 / person
